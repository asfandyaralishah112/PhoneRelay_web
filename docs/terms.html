1. The service

PhoneRelay lets the phone you carry place and receive calls through a separate relay phone that holds your SIM cards, over your own Wi-Fi network. The app runs on iPhone and Android; the relay phone runs a relay module that we provision to your account.

The service depends on your network, your relay phone staying powered and online, and your mobile carrier. We do not provide telephony and we do not control call quality, coverage or whether a carrier connects a call.

3. Plans and payment

Relay needs an active plan. We offer a trial, monthly, yearly and lifetime plans. Plans are activated by our team after payment is agreed; the app does not process card details.

  • Trial - a fixed number of relayed calls. When they are used, relay stops until a plan is activated.
  • Monthly - 30 days of relay access, renewed on request.
  • Yearly - 365 days of relay access.
  • Lifetime - relay access for as long as we offer the service. If we discontinue it, we will give reasonable notice.

Prices, renewal and refunds are agreed with you before payment. A plan that has expired or a trial that is exhausted refuses the next call; a call already in progress is never cut off.

4. One relay phone at a time

A plan covers one relay phone and one phone at a time. If two phones stay connected to the same account, the app warns you. If both remain connected for seven continuous days, the account is blocked until it is resolved. Use Use only this phone in Settings to keep the phone you are holding and disconnect the other.
