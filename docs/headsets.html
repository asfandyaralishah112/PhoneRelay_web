Audio and headsets

Relayed calls use your phone's normal audio route. There is nothing to register.

When a relayed call connects, the audio is handled by the phone in your hand exactly like a normal call. PhoneRelay does not lock audio to a particular device, and there is no allow-list to manage.

Where the sound comes out

RouteHow to use it
Earpiece The default. Hold the phone to your ear as usual.
Speaker Tap the speaker button on the call screen. Tap it again to go back.
Bluetooth headset or car kit Connect it in the phone's Bluetooth settings before or during a call. Any headset that supports hands-free calling works; the phone routes audio to it.
Wired headset Plug it in. The phone switches to it automatically.

If a headset misbehaves

  • Disconnect and reconnect it in Bluetooth settings, then try the call again.
  • Tap the speaker button twice to force the route back to the phone, then reconnect the headset.
  • Check the phone's microphone permission for PhoneRelay - a denied microphone makes you inaudible even though the call connects.
  • Some car kits take a few seconds to claim the audio route after a call connects. Give it a moment before switching manually.

Call audio is never recorded or uploaded. Bluetooth is only used for your headset - the relay between your two phones runs over Wi-Fi.
