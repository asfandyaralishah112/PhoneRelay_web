Getting started

  1. Install PhoneRelay on the phone you carry (iPhone or Android).
  2. Open Settings and sign in with Apple, or enter the activation code we sent you.
  3. We activate your plan (trial, monthly, yearly or lifetime) on that account.
  4. In Settings > Wi-Fi SIM phone, enter the address of your relay phone (the pairing code is filled in automatically). Both phones must be on the same Wi-Fi network.
  5. Open the keypad, pick the SIM, and dial.

Common questions

Why does the app say “Not enabled”?

The Wi-Fi SIM phone section is always visible, but pairing needs an active plan. If it says “Not enabled”, sign in again, enter your activation code, or write to us and we will check the plan on your account.

What do I need to use PhoneRelay?

  • An iPhone on iOS 26.2 or later, or an Android phone on Android 7 or later.
  • A relay phone - a rooted Android phone running the PhoneRelay relay module, with your SIM cards in it. We prepare and provision it to your account.
  • Both phones on the same Wi-Fi network.
  • An Apple sign-in or an activation code with an active plan.

Does it work when I am away from home?

Not today. The relay link runs over your local network, so the phone you carry and the relay phone must be on the same Wi-Fi. Calls to and from the SIM still use the normal mobile network through the relay phone.

It says two phones are using this account

A plan covers one phone at a time. When a second phone is detected, the app asks at launch; choose Use this iPhone on the phone you want to keep. If two phones stay active for seven continuous days, the account is blocked until it is resolved.

My trial ran out mid-call

It will not cut a call that is already connected. An exhausted trial or an expired plan refuses the next call. Write to us to move to a monthly, yearly or lifetime plan.

Do you record calls?

No. Call audio passes between your two phones and is never recorded, stored or uploaded. See the Privacy Policy for the short list of what we do hold.

Which SIM is used?

You choose. The keypad's SIM selector lists the SIMs in the relay phone; the choice applies to that call. The selected SIM is shown in Settings too.

Can I change or cancel a plan?

Write to us. Monthly and yearly plans are renewed on request, so nothing renews without you agreeing to it. Lifetime plans do not expire while the service runs.

How do I delete my account?

Email us from the account you use in PhoneRelay. The deletion page lists exactly what is removed.

Troubleshooting

The relay phone will not connect

  • Check both phones are on the same Wi-Fi network - not one on mobile data and one on Wi-Fi, and not a guest network that isolates clients.
  • Check the relay phone is powered on and its relay module is running.
  • Re-enter the relay phone's local address in Settings > Wi-Fi SIM phone; the address can change after a router restart unless it is fixed in your router.
  • Turn the relay phone's Wi-Fi off and on, then try again.

No audio, or audio on the wrong speaker

  • Use the speaker button on the call screen to switch between the earpiece and the speaker.
  • If a Bluetooth headset is connected, the phone's normal audio routing applies; disconnect it to return to the earpiece.
  • Check the microphone permission is allowed for PhoneRelay.

Sign-in fails

  • Check the phone has internet access, then try again.
  • Update to the latest build - older builds point at retired servers.
  • Send us the exact message shown; we can look up the attempt on our side.

Sending diagnostics

In the app, Settings > Diagnostics can share a technical log with us. It contains build and connection details, never call audio. Share it when reporting a fault, together with what you were doing at the time.

Emergency calls: do not rely on PhoneRelay for emergency calls. Keep a normal phone able to call emergency services.
