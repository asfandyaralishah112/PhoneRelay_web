Calls over Wi-Fi

Your SIM, on the phone you actually carry.

PhoneRelay relays calls from the SIM cards in your relay phone to your iPhone or Android phone, over your own Wi-Fi. No Bluetooth pairing, no forwarding numbers, no call audio stored.

Three steps, then it just works

The relay phone holds the SIMs and stays on your network. Your phone does the dialling.

1

Sign in with Google

Your account carries the plan and binds the relay phone to you. The same account works on iPhone and Android, one relay phone at a time.

2

Add your relay phone

It sits on the same Wi-Fi with your SIM cards in it. Enter its address in Settings once and PhoneRelay keeps the link open.

3

Dial as usual

Pick which SIM should make the call, then dial from the keypad. The call screen gives you mute, speaker, keypad and end.

Everything a phone call needs

Built as a real dialer, not a forwarding trick.

A dialer, not a bridge

Recents, contacts, favourites and a keypad - the screens you already know, with calls carried by your relay phone.

Pick the SIM

Two, three, as many as the relay phone holds. Choose the line for each call from the keypad's SIM selector.

iPhone and Android

The same account and plan on both. Sign in on the phone in your hand; the relay phone stays where the signal is good.

Wi-Fi, not Bluetooth

No pairing, no range games. If both phones are on your network, the relay works - across the house or across the office.

In-call controls

Mute, speaker, keypad and end - with the timer starting the moment the other side answers.

Nothing recorded

Call audio passes through and is never written to a file or sent to a server. Contacts and call history stay on your phone.

Plans

Lifetime

Plans are activated by our team after payment - the app never handles card details. Relay is hidden until a plan is active; the rest of the app keeps working.

What you need

Your phone

iPhone on iOS 26.2 or later, or Android 7 or later. Signed in with the Google account that holds your plan.

A relay phone

A rooted Android phone running the PhoneRelay relay module, with your SIM cards in it, powered on and on the same Wi-Fi.

An active plan

Trial, monthly, yearly or lifetime. We set the relay phone up and provision it to your account before you start.
