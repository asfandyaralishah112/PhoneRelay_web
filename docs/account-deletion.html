Delete your PhoneRelay account

PhoneRelay - com.LabNexus.phonerelay

Deleting your account removes your email address, plan and every record we hold about your phones. It cannot be undone, and relay stops working on every device signed in with that account.

How to request deletion

  1. Email asfandyaralishah@rocketmail.com.
  2. Send it from the Google account you use in PhoneRelay, so we can identify the account. Write from that address - we cannot action a request from a different address.
  3. Use the subject Delete my PhoneRelay account. Tell us whether you are also stopping the relay phone, if you have one.

We action requests within 7 days and reply once the account is gone. If you no longer have the relay phone, say so - that helps us close it cleanly.

Signing out is not deletion. Signing out only clears the session on that phone; your account and plan stay.

What is deleted

DataWhat happens
Your account and Google email address Deleted permanently.
Plan, expiry and trial usage Deleted permanently.
Device records: the phone ids, platform, model and OS version, and the relay module key id and fingerprint Deleted permanently. The relay phone is unbound, so it no longer works with that account.
Relay call records (call id, start time, duration, whether counted) and diagnostics uploads Deleted permanently.
The session and device token stored on your phone Cleared when you delete the app, sign out, or delete the account.

What was never stored

Your contacts, call history, the numbers you dial, SIM details and call audio are used only on your own phones. None of it is uploaded, so there is nothing of that kind for us to delete.

After deletion

Relay stops on every phone signed in with the account, and your relay phone is released. The dialer still opens, but without a plan it cannot place relayed calls. You can sign in again at any time to start a new account; a new plan and a newly provisioned relay phone would be needed.

Questions

Write to asfandyaralishah@rocketmail.com, or see the Privacy Policy for what the service collects.
