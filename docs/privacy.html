Privacy Policy

PhoneRelay - com.LabNexus.phonerelay - last updated 13 November 2026

PhoneRelay relays calls from a relay phone that holds your SIM cards to the iPhone or Android phone you carry. This policy explains what the app and our service collect, what they deliberately do not, and how to delete what we hold.

In short: we hold the email address of the Google account you sign in with, your plan, identifiers for the two phones, a record of relayed calls (time and length, not the number dialled) and technical diagnostics. Your contacts, call history, SIM details and call audio stay on your own phones.

What we collect

Data Why we have it
Google account email address, and the account id our authentication provider creates for it This is your account. It identifies which plan belongs to you and which relay phone is bound to it. Sign-in itself is handled by Google; we never see your password.
Phone identifiers: a random device id created on first sign-in, the platform (iOS or Android), and your device model and OS version To enforce one relay phone per account, recognise your phone on later launches, and investigate sign-in or relay problems.
Relay phone identifiers: the key id of the relay module and a fingerprint of the relay phone it runs on To bind the relay phone to your account and to detect a copied or cloned relay module, which protects your account.
Plan and relay usage: plan type, start and expiry, trial calls used, and one record per relayed call containing a random call id, the start time, the duration, and whether the call counted against the plan To run the plan, charge trial calls and answer billing and support questions. Call records do not contain the number dialled, the contact, or any audio.
Technical diagnostics, kept on your phone and shared only when you choose: app version and build, OS version, device model, connection state, error messages and recent in-app log lines To find and fix faults. The app does not upload diagnostics automatically; you can share the log from Settings. A log line can include a phone number or a local network address when that is the thing that failed.
IP address, seen by our database provider when the app makes a request To deliver the request and protect the service from abuse. We do not store it as part of your account.

That is the complete list. PhoneRelay has no advertising, no analytics SDK, no advertising identifier and no third-party tracking.

What stays on your phones

Several permissions are needed to make and show calls. The data behind them is used on the device and is never uploaded to us or anyone else:

DataUsed for
ContactsShowing names instead of bare numbers in the dialer and call screen.
Call historyShowing recents, and adding calls made through the relay phone so your history is complete.
Phone numbers you dialPlacing the call through the relay phone. They are not sent to our servers.
SIM detailsLetting you choose which SIM in the relay phone places the call.
Microphone audio and call audioCarrying your voice and the other side's voice during a relayed call. Audio is never recorded, stored or sent to a server.
Local network address of the relay phoneConnecting to it over your Wi-Fi. It stays on your network.

What we never do

  • We do not record calls, and we cannot listen to them.
  • We do not read or upload your contacts, messages or call history.
  • We do not use the microphone in the background.
  • We do not sell your data, and we do not share it for advertising.

Who processes your data

  • Google - handles sign-in. We receive your email address and account id from Google; Google never receives your contacts or call audio from us.
  • Supabase - hosts the database and authentication service that store your account, plan, device records, call records and diagnostics.

These are service providers acting on our behalf. We may also disclose data where the law requires it, or to protect the service and its users from abuse. Data may be processed in countries other than yours by these providers.

Security

All traffic between the app and our server uses HTTPS. Each phone holds a device token after sign-in; the server stores only a hash of that token. The database tables are not reachable from the app directly - every read and write goes through a small set of functions that check the token first. Sign-in tokens are kept in the app's private storage.

Retention

Your account, plan, device records and call records are kept until you delete your account. Diagnostics stay on your phone; if you choose to share a log with us, it is kept only long enough to resolve the issue.

Deleting your data

In the app: Settings > Delete account. Or email asfandyaralishah@rocketmail.com from the account you signed in with, with the subject Delete my PhoneRelay account. Requests are actioned within 7 days, and usually much sooner. See the account deletion page for the exact steps and for what is removed.

Deleting the account removes your email address, plan, device records, call records and diagnostics. Relay stops working on every phone signed in with that account. Signing out, by contrast, only clears the session on that phone.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of the data we hold about your account, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Write to the address above from the email address on the account. We answer within 30 days.

Children

PhoneRelay is not directed at children and is intended for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will be updated. Material changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
