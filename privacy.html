PhoneRelay — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026 · Operated by LabNexus

PhoneRelay lets an iPhone place and receive calls through a paired SIM phone on the same Wi-Fi network. This page explains what the app collects and why.

1. What we collect

  • Account. The email address returned by Sign in with Apple (or Google), an account identifier, a device identifier, a relay device token, and your plan and usage counters.
  • Calls. A call identifier, start time and duration, used to count plan usage. Call audio is relayed directly between your iPhone and your paired SIM phone over your local network. We do not record or store call audio.
  • Contacts. Read on the device to show contacts, favourites and number suggestions. They are not uploaded.
  • Diagnostics. If you choose to send diagnostics, we receive app logs and device state. They do not include call audio.

2. How we use it

To operate the relay, authenticate your device, enforce plans and trial limits, prevent abuse, and provide support.

3. Sharing

We use Supabase for database and authentication hosting, and Apple or Google for sign-in. We do not sell personal data.

4. Retention and deletion

Account and usage records are kept until you delete your account. You can delete the account in the app: Settings → Delete account. This removes the account and its relay data.

5. Security

Traffic to our backend uses TLS. Credentials on the iPhone are stored in the Keychain. The local pairing between the iPhone and the SIM phone is authenticated with a challenge-response using the pairing token.

6. Children

PhoneRelay is not directed to children under 13.
