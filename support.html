PhoneRelay — Support

Operated by LabNexus

Setup

  1. On the SIM phone: install the PhoneRelay Magisk module and enroll it with your provider.
  2. On the iPhone: install PhoneRelay and sign in with Apple, or enter the activation code you received.
  3. Open Settings → Pair / connect SIM phone and enter the pairing code, or let the app find the SIM phone automatically.
  4. Dial from the keypad and pick the SIM phone's SIM in the SIM selector.

If the SIM phone is not found

  • Both phones must be on the same Wi-Fi network (or the SIM phone's hotspot), and the relay must be enabled on the SIM phone.
  • Check that the relay module is running on the SIM phone. If you enrolled from a PC, use the enrollment app's Verify setup button.
  • Enter the SIM phone's IP address manually in the pairing screen.

Account and plans

Sign in with the same account on the iPhone. If your plan has expired or the account is blocked, the app shows the status under Settings. Contact us if the status looks wrong.
